A square record cover, photographic. The look of a hand-tinted silver-gelatin photograph from the 1920s: warm sepia, amber and faded oxblood, soft film grain, deep velvety shadows, slight vignetting, printed on heavy matte card. Into this period print, something from a century later is blended as described, lit cold electric blue and magenta, rendered in the same grain and print texture so it belongs to the photograph rather than sitting on top of it. Leave the upper third of the frame quieter than the rest, with room for a title. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks.
